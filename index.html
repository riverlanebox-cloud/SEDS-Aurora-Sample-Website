Mission brief

Making space exploration
everyone's hobby.

SEDS Aurora is the VIT-AP chapter of SEDS India, a student-run organisation that promotes the exploration and development of space.

We're working to make space exploration a popular hobby — and to show students it's a viable career. SEDS gives you an organised pathway to start real space projects, break away from the usual class-work, and take your first step towards a career beyond the sky.

From guest lectures with astrophysicists to rover builds and late-night debates about settling Mars, Aurora is where curious people come to launch.
